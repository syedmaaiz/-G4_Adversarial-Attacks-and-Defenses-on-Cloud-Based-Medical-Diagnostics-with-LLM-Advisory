Executive Summary

The model is useful on clean images but vulnerable to FGSM. Defensive randomization helps slightly; adversarial training is the recommended defense.

Recommended defense: adversarial training It raised adversarial accuracy from 53.69% to 74.04% and reduced standard attack success from 37.38% to 13.81%, while clean accuracy stayed essentially unchanged.
